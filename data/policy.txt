STORE RETURN, WARRANTY AND SHIPPING POLICY

Clothing items, including jackets, can be returned within 30 days of the purchase date. Items must be unused, have original tags attached, and include the original receipt.

Electronics, including wireless earbuds, can be returned within 14 days of the purchase date. Items must include original accessories and packaging.

All purchased products have a 365-day manufacturer warranty from the purchase date for manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is not covered.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after an order is shipped.
